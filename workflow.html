Publishing Workflow Stages

Stage 1: Manuscript Submission

The author submits the complete book draft along with the synopsis, genre details, and author biography.

Key Action: Initial document formatting and verification check.

Stage 2: Editing

The assigned editor reviews the manuscript for structure, character development, narrative flow, and clarity.

Key Action: Developmental and line editing with suggested author revisions.

Stage 3: Proofreading

The proofreader conducts a thorough check for grammatical errors, spelling mistakes, punctuation, and typesetting.

Key Action: Final grammar correction and text formatting.

Stage 4: Cover Design

The design team creates the front cover, back cover, spine artwork, and internal typography layout.

Key Action: Graphic artwork approval and print-ready layout creation.

Stage 5: Review

Senior reviewers and the editorial board perform a final review of the formatted book proof before printing.

Key Action: Quality assurance and editorial sign-off.

Stage 6: Publication

The final book is assigned an ISBN, printed in physical form, and published across digital bookstores.

Key Action: ISBN registration, printing, and global distribution.
